# Extend the SystemPAK packaging models

Start a Terra chat in the `oomp_packaging` project and paste the following prompt.

> Work through the remaining SystemPAK records already in this packaging catalogue, one SKU at a time. Begin with rectangular products sharing the SKU 209 footprint, then handle other rectangular, square and round families. Preserve the existing 209 reference. Read its generated part README, models/systempak/209.json, models/systempak_rectangle.scad, systempak_model.py and the rectangle population helper first.
>
> For each stored OOMP ID, open its official SystemPAK product listing, its matching SKU technical sheet and at least one SKU-specific product photograph. Inspect the actual image. Reconcile body-only and closed dimensions, check units, orientation and footprint, and document conflicting figures and photo-derived dimensions separately. The listed capacity is a product identifier, not a linear scale factor. Do not transfer an undocumented feature or dimension between SKUs by guessing. If the outline or dimensions cannot be established, log that SKU and the missing evidence, and continue with the next one.
>
> Put each supported rectangular SKU in models/systempak/<SKU>.json and reuse the 209 profile where the actual construction matches. Keep material thickness, top radius, wall draft, collar, foot and tab independently adjustable. Add a new parameter or family when the photos show different construction. Round and handled tubs need matching profile generators, validators and registration in their own population helper. Preserve the existing OOMP IDs. Keep bare body and removable lid as separate watertight solids in millimetres, in their shared assembled coordinates. Carry official source links, SKU photo, stated dimensions and estimated details into the generated model.json and part README.
>
> For each part run python action_make_all.py --part <canonical_OOMP_ID>. Verify that its population record selects the profile, both solids are watertight, their bounds reproduce the source dimensions, the seated pieces have no unintended overlap, and the lid and tab appear correctly in the rendered portrait. Open the render and compare it to the source photo. Confirm that the normal packaging make-all picks up the same registration. Record passed and evidence-blocked SKUs with source links. Finish with a contact sheet and paths to the generated parts. Leave the drawer project alone unless explicitly asked to import another SKU.
