Description

20 Litre Round Clear Tamper Evident Plastic Container with Lid – 200mm Diameter

Our 20L Round Clear Tamper Evident Containers with Lids are a secure, high-quality, and economical packaging solution — ideal for storing and transporting bulk goods, ingredients, or bait.

✅ Tamper Evident Security
Each container features a built-in tamper tab, offering added protection during transit and storage. The lid cannot be removed without breaking the tab, giving your customers confidence in the product’s freshness and integrity.

✅ Leak-Proof & Liquid-Tight
Designed to hold both solids and liquids, these containers are 100% leak-free — making them suitable for a wide range of uses from bait and seeds to wholesale food ingredients, sauces, or pastes.

✅ Clear, Food-Grade Plastic
Made from durable, transparent plastic to showcase contents, and manufactured to ISO and BRC food safety standards, ensuring hygiene and compliance at every stage.

Print Options

There are three main options available if you require the container to display an image, design, or label of your choosing, these are:

IML – In-Mould Labelling (IML) is when a label is inserted into the container during manufacturing. This method is ideal for products like snack tubs and soups.

  • Minimum Order Quantity = 25,000. The print order can include a variety of designs.
  • For a bespoke IML quote please fill out our form

 

Self-Adhesive – Self-adhesive labels are stickers that are designed and shaped to your desired spec and are fantastic for items like our salad trays and deli pots.

  • Minimum Order Quantity = 1,000. The print order can include a full-colour photo within the design.
  • For a bespoke Self Adhesive label quote please fill out our form
  • For a detailed look into self-adhesive options please see our guide here

 

Cardboard Sleeves – Cardboard Sleeves are a great way to display a variety of information on your products like branding and nutritional information.

  • Minimum Order Quantity = 1,000.
  • For a bespoke Cardboard Sleeve quote please fill out our form
  • For a detailed look into cardboard-sleeve options please see our guide here
